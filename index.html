Regulation Facts

Confirmed FIA 2026 technical changes

Smaller Chassis

Maximum wheelbase drops to 3400 mm (from 3600 mm) and width to 1900 mm (from 2000 mm).

Lighter Cars

Minimum mass target reduced by 30 kg to 768 kg, improving agility and raceability.

Power Unit Shift

Electrical output from the MGU-K rises to 350 kW while the MGU-H is removed.

100% Sustainable Fuel

New power unit regulations require use of 100% advanced sustainable fuels.

Active Aero Modes

Cars switch between Z mode (downforce) and X mode (low drag) for straights and racing phases.

Calendar Structure

24 rounds are regionally grouped to reduce travel impact, with Madrid joining and Barcelona retained.
